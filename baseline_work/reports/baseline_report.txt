1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-------------------
This report establishes an independent, reproducible, and leakage-audited benchmark evaluation of existing pretrained image forensics baselines on diffusion-inpainted imagery from the OpenSDID dataset (Wang et al., 2025). The evaluation strictly adheres to double-blind inference protocols: models receive only a single inspected test image without access to original unedited images, ground-truth masks, prompt text, or class labels.

A total of 120 images (20 validation, 100 test across 5 diffusion generators: SD1.5, SD2.1, SDXL, SD3, and Flux.1) were frozen into auditable manifests with full SHA256 hashes, ground-truth mask verification, and area stratification. A cross-split SHA256 hash audit verified 0% data overlap between training/validation and held-out test splits.

Key Empirical Findings:
- Operational Baseline: Visual Anomaly Assessment System (VAAS v2-base-df2023; MIT License).
- Overall Macro Detection Accuracy across 5 generators: 46.00% (Balanced Accuracy: 46.00%, F1: 46.79%, ROC-AUC: 44.20%).
- Overall Macro Localization Mean IoU on edited test images: 16.33% (Mean Pixel F1: 23.13%).
- Critical Failure Mode on Tiny Edits (<2% area, N=5): Mean IoU drops to 0.95% (Mean F1: 1.83%), demonstrating that standard global feature extractors almost completely miss localized small edits.
- False Alarm Rate on Clean Photographs (N=50 genuine images): VAAS falsely flags an average of 16.51% of clean pixels as anomalous, exposing severe vulnerability to false positives on unmanipulated camera images.
- Comparator Status: FUSED (Nuzhdin et al., 2026) weights and code are available, but full training is blocked on local hardware due to 2x H100 requirements; single-image inference is feasible. TruFor requires implicit agreement to custom terms and was not downloaded. IFA-Net released no code or weights.

3. BASELINE CANDIDATE SELECTION & ACCESS AUDIT
---------------------------------------------
A. VAAS (v2-base-df2023) — OPERATIONAL & BENCHMARKED
   - Repository: https://github.com/OBA-Research/VAAS
   - Checkpoint: Hugging Face OBA-Research/vaas (revision v2-base-df2023)
   - Architecture: SegFormer (PatchConsistencySegformer) + ViT (FxViT)
   - License: MIT License
   - Preprocessing: 224x224 RGB, ImageNet mean/std normalization.
   - Outputs: Raw patch-consistency sigmoid map (224x224) and scalar hybrid score S_H.
   - Documented Score Polarity Divergence: In vaas==0.2.0, hybrid_score.py defines S_P = 1.0 - mean(pred_sig) and S_F = exp(-delta/sigma), meaning both are fidelity/clean scores (lower = more anomalous / fake). In contrast, the VAAS preprint (arXiv:2512.15512) describes S_F as an anomaly deviation (higher = anomalous). On our frozen validation set (val_sd15), lower_is_fake achieves ROC-AUC 0.47 vs 0.53 for higher_is_fake, showing near-chance discriminability on diffusion inpainting. All raw scores are preserved in per_image.csv to prevent loss of information.

B. FUSED (Nuzhdin et al., 2026) — CODE & WEIGHTS AUDITED
   - Repository: https://github.com/AntonNuzhdin/FUSED
   - Checkpoints: Hugging Face aonuzhdin/FUSED (fused_opensdi.pth ~306 MB, sparsevit_pretrained.pth ~193 MB)
   - License: MIT License
   - Architecture: Forensic SparseViT + frozen CLIP-ConvNeXt-XXLarge (convnext_xxlarge.clip_laion2b_soup from timm) with MoE fusion.

4. DATASET DESIGN & ZERO-LEAKAGE AUDIT
--------------------------------------
Primary Benchmark: OpenSDID (Wang et al., 2025; arXiv:2503.19653; CC BY-SA 4.0).
Real images sourced from Megalith-10M. Manipulations comprise localized inpainting edits (partial) with ground-truth masks.

A. Frozen Splits:
1. Validation Set (val_sd15):
   - Source: nebula/OpenSDI_train (sd15 split)
   - 20 images total: 10 genuine real (label 0), 10 diffusion-inpainted fake (label 1).
   - Edit area strata: 4 samples <2%, 4 samples 2–10%, 2 samples >10%.
   - Role: Exclusively for threshold selection and polarity calibration. NEVER used for reporting paper performance.
2. Independent Test Set (100 images, 20 per generator):
   - Source: nebula/OpenSDI_test
   - In-domain split: test_sd15 (10 real, 10 fake)
   - Unseen cross-generator splits:
     - test_sd2: SD2.1 (10 real, 10 fake)
     - test_sdxl: SDXL (10 real, 10 fake)
     - test_sd3: SD3 (10 real, 10 fake)
     - test_flux: Flux.1 (10 real, 10 fake)
   - Total Test: 50 real, 50 fake (balanced 1:1 ratio).
   - Test Area Strata across 50 fake images: 5 samples <2%, 21 samples 2–10%, 24 samples >10%.

B. Integrity & Leakage Audit:
- SHA256 checksums computed for every image and mask asset.
- Strict Cross-Split Collision Test: Passed. 0 duplicate image hashes between val_sd15 and any test split.
- Mask Semantics: Grayscale values binarized at >127, matching official OpenSDID / FUSED dataset protocols.
- Dimension check: 100% of masks match image dimensions (e.g. 1024x768, 1024x683, etc.).
- Mask Resizing: Nearest-neighbor interpolation (PIL.Image.NEAREST) to preserve hard binary boundaries without introducing false antialiased intermediate values.

7. DETAILED FAILURE MODE ANALYSIS
---------------------------------
Visual failure case overlays are generated and preserved in baseline_work\reports\failure_cases\:

1. Tiny Edit Localization Failure (failure_tiny_edit_missed.png):
   - Sample: sd15_010005_partial_sd15_fake_003718562.jpg
   - Edit Area: 1.85% of image (14,513 pixels out of 786,432 pixels).
   - Ground Truth: Inpainted small object in natural scene.
   - Outcome: IoU = 0.0000, Pixel F1 = 0.0000.
   - Forensic Cause: ViT patch embeddings (14x14 or 16x16 tokens) downsample high-frequency local boundaries. Small edits are averaged out in the global self-attention layers, leaving the anomaly probability below the 0.5 detection threshold.

2. Large Inpainting Semantic Miss (failure_large_edit_missed.png):
   - Sample: sd3_010006_partial_sd3_fake_001956282.jpg
   - Edit Area: 10.57% of image (73,912 pixels).
   - Outcome: IoU = 0.0000, Pixel F1 = 0.0000.
   - Forensic Cause: Modern flow-matching diffusion generators (SD3 / Flux) produce sharp semantic coherence that lacks classic resampling/color-space artifacts, escaping general patch consistency heads.

3. Genuine Image False Alarm (failure_clean_image_false_alarm.png):
   - Sample: sd3_015004_partial_sd3_real_001975819.jpg
   - GT Label: 0 (Authentic camera photograph from Megalith-10M).
   - False Alarm Area: 42.73% of image falsely predicted as manipulated!
   - Forensic Cause: High-contrast natural textures, depth-of-field blur, and lighting gradients trigger high patch-inconsistency scores in SegFormer, demonstrating that general anomaly baselines cannot differentiate natural scene depth from synthetic inpainting.

8. SCIENTIFIC COMPARISON READINESS & BENCHMARK GAPS
---------------------------------------------------
A. Can EchoSeam-Net be compared against this baseline?
   YES, conditionally:
   1. The protocol established here provides a fair, leakage-free benchmark on 100 held-out OpenSDID images spanning 5 generators.
   2. EchoSeam-Net or any proposed detector can now run against the exact frozen manifests in baseline_work\data_manifests\ using the identical evaluation metric code (baseline_work\code\test_metrics_and_checks.py).
   3. A valid claim of superiority requires EchoSeam-Net to outperform:
      - Macro Localization IoU > 16.33%
      - Tiny Edit (<2%) IoU > 0.95%
      - Clean Image False Alarm Area < 16.51%
      - Detection Balanced Accuracy > 46.00%
   4. No paper claim of SOTA or superiority may be made until EchoSeam-Net is actually implemented, trained, and evaluated under this exact protocol.

B. Known Scientific Limitations:
   1. VAAS is a general visual anomaly baseline trained on DF2023, not a dedicated inpainting specialist. Its poor performance reflects domain mismatch, not necessarily incompetence in general anomaly tasks.
   2. Test set size is N=100 (20 per generator). While bootstrap 95% confidence intervals are reported, expanding to larger sample sizes (e.g. 1,000+ images) will narrow confidence intervals for publication.
   3. FUSED comparison remains at the level of published results (44.5% IoU on OpenSDID) and single-image validation; full local retraining of FUSED was not performed due to the 2x H100 requirement.
