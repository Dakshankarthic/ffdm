The manuscript content and table values were retained at the author's request. Only table formatting and one missing equation label were changed.

Its accompanying counts TP=46, TN=47, FP=3, FN=4 yield 93.00% accuracy, 93.00% balanced accuracy, and 92.93% positive-class F1 if they are the same 100 predictions. Confirm whether different populations or runs were used.

A completed 10K run checkpoint, training logs, full test manifest, per-image predictions and aggregation specification were not located in the workspace. They may exist elsewhere. The paper currently describes these values as achieved; obtain and verify the run artifacts.

3. MEAN IoU - The five supplied generator IoUs (68.4, 56.1, 52.7, 41.8, 29.5) average 49.70% with equal generator weighting, not 47.30%. Different sample weights could explain the difference; aggregation needs documentation.

4. FUSED COMPARISON - The FUSED paper reports OpenSDID image accuracy/F1 at 0.5 and localization IoU/per-image pixel F1 averaged over generators with SD1.5-only training. Its main OpenSDID tables do not report the extra balanced accuracy, AUROC and false positive rates shown in the manuscript; find actual sources. Model comparisons require matching dataset, split, thresholds, weighting and metric definitions. FUSED's per-image pixel F1 is not directly comparable with pooled-pixel F1.
