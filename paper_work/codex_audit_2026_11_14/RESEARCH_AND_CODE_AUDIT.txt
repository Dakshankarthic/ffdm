CONCLUSION
The current results do not establish a successful AI-edit localizer. The
implementation contains useful model components, but the research training
entry point currently always generates synthetic data. Reported synthetic
training-set metrics must not be described as real-image validation.

IMPLEMENTATION FINDINGS
1. controlled_localization/src/controlled_localization/train.py lines 239-267:
run_training_experiment accepts manifest_path and schedule_path but does not
read either; it calls create_overfit_dataset unconditionally. This is a
critical blocker for real-data training and controlled comparisons.
2. overfit_check.py lines 38-57 creates random-noise images and inserts a
random-noise square. The model fits these examples, not verified AI-edited
photographs. train.py evaluates the same generated manifest used for training;
its field named validation_macro_iou is an in-sample synthetic metric.
3. Checkpoint save does not include RNG state or sampler cursor, and the
resume loader does not restore those; exact continuation is not established.
4. data/pairs.py silently resizes mismatched masks. This can hide geometry
errors: reject such pairs unless a verified transform is explicitly recorded.
5. canonicalize.py converts image mode to RGB, but does not perform the
claimed ICC-managed conversion to sRGB. Preserve and inspect color metadata.
6. Existing automated suite independently rerun: 17 passed in 40.48 seconds.
It checks useful component contracts but does not establish real-data loading,
source-disjoint validation, correct resume, or research readiness.

SAVED BENCHMARK: RECOMPUTED FROM EXISTING CSV, NOT A NEW INFERENCE RUN
100 images, 50 edited, 50 genuine; only 5 tiny edits. Recorded T2O AUROC
0.5348, mean edited IoU 0.04445, mean tiny IoU 0.05726. Average fraction of
genuine-image pixels marked positive: 4.4183%. At the reported image
decision threshold ALL 50 genuine and ALL 50 edited samples were marked
positive: image-level false-positive rate = 100%, sensitivity = 100%.
Pixel-area false positives and image-level false positives are different.
These are exploratory results on already-inspected viewer-derived assets;
mask polarity/semantics and source independence remain unverified. Tiny-edit
results from five examples do not substantiate a performance claim. Baseline
comparisons require matching saved predictions and mask evaluation; do not
claim superiority from the current summary. Do not reuse this set as a fresh
unseen final test after inspecting it to develop the model.

DATA AUDIT
The six baseline manifests contain 120 records total, 20 each; pilot_train
contains another 16. All image files in these manifests exist and recorded
image hashes match where provided. Every edited mask inspected is a JPEG
with values beyond 0 and 255; treating white as edited remains an assumption.
Each baseline manifest lists 10 positive and 10 negative records; five tiny
edits in the 100 test-labelled images and four in validation, using >127 as
the unverified mask threshold. No original-image path is present in the
inspected manifests. No exact image-hash or declared-source-ID overlap was
found across these seven manifests. This is not proof against near-duplicate
scenes, misleading IDs, provenance leakage or pretraining overlap.
Machine-readable evidence and script: asset_audit.json and audit_saved_assets.py.

NOVELTY ASSESSMENT
ProCoS already trains manipulation localization with clean/degraded view
alignment and teacher/student KL; its domain is industrial inspection images
and it also uses prototype learning. Our proposed method uses a detached view
of the same model rather than its EMA teacher, and targets small AI edits.
Those differences alone do not establish a novel contribution.
https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/12/3647
INP-X already studies original-background restoration and training against
global artifacts: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00192
FUSED already combines forensic and semantic evidence and evaluates artifact
dependence: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28302
GAP-SAM examines domain alignment and boundary shortcuts:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20929
IFA-Net uses reconstruction and iterative refinement:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18842
Do not claim clean/degraded consistency, local-global feature fusion, paired
controls or tiny-region evaluation as independently new. A narrower empirical
study comparing these mechanisms in a controlled AI-inpainting setting may be
viable if strong baselines, scale, uncertainty and new evidence justify it.

PRACTICAL DATA DIRECTION
For T1 versus T2, A->B and C->D consistency only requires an image and its
processed counterpart; the genuine A and edited C need not be paired with each
other. This permits a separately documented T1/T2 design on verified unpaired
genuine and edited images. T2O outside-edit A->C consistency DOES require a
verified aligned original/edit pair and a trustworthy mask. Change the
protocol explicitly before comparing this alternative with the earlier design.
Candidate paired resource: INP-X authors list original, inpainted and exchanged
images at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/emirhanbilgic/inpainting-exchange/data
Check access, licenses, actual mask availability, scene grouping, artifact and
split provenance before using it. SAGI is another candidate:
https://mever-team.github.io/SAGI/ . No dataset access or suitability is
claimed here without inspecting its assets and terms.
