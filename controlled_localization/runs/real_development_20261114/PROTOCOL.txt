Real-image development protocol v1.2 — 2026-11-14
T1 versus T2: same pretrained architecture, seed, sampled images, crops,
compression recipes and optimizer budget. T2 adds within-image directed KL.
Unedited/edited photographs need not share a scene. Outside-region loss is
disabled. This explicitly differs from the original four-view paired design.
Official OpenSDI hf_datasets.py confirms mean RGB mask >127.5 as positive.
Viewer-transcoded assets remain a development limitation; this experiment
does not certify original-byte forensic fidelity or a publication benchmark.
320 candidate rows from 8 blocks of the official training split. Exclude
earlier baseline/pilot source IDs, deduplicate image hashes, split source IDs
by deterministic SHA256 modulo 5 into training and validation. This is not
a random representative dataset and does not prove near-duplicate exclusion.
No old test benchmark is used. Validation is development, not final testing.
Checkpoint selection: maximum validation mean edited-image IoU at 0.5.
Report detection AUROC and genuine-image positive area separately. Do not
claim high accuracy until independent unseen-image evaluation supports it.
Initial budget: 400 optimizer steps per variant, four 384px tiles per update.
Source license: CC BY-SA 4.0, academic-use notice on official dataset page.
Mask conversion provenance and downloaded viewer bytes remain on disk.
